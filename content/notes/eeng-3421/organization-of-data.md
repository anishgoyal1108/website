## Frequency and Relative Frequency {#frequency-and-relative-frequency}

<div class="callout theorem-callout theorem-callout-definition theorem-callout-en theorem-callout-mathwiki" data-callout="definition" data-theorem-index="23.2">
<div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-icon"><svg class="svg-inline--fa" viewBox="0 0 448 512"><use href="#cicon-fa-book"/></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner"><span class="theorem-callout-main-title">Definition 23.2</span> <span class="theorem-callout-subtitle">(Frequency Distribution of a Qualitative Variable)</span>.</div></div>
<div class="callout-content">

A frequency distribution of a qualitative variable lists all categories and the number of elements that belong to each category

</div>
</div>

<div class="callout theorem-callout theorem-callout-proposition theorem-callout-en theorem-callout-mathwiki" data-callout="proposition" data-theorem-index="23.3">
<div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-icon"><svg class="svg-inline--fa" viewBox="0 0 448 512"><use href="#cicon-fa-calculator"/></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner"><span class="theorem-callout-main-title">Proposition 23.3</span> <span class="theorem-callout-subtitle">(Relative Frequency)</span>.</div></div>
<div class="callout-content">

Relative frequency of a category equals

$$
\text{relative frequency} = \frac{\text{frequency of that category}}{\text{sum of all frequencies}}
$$

</div>
</div>

<div class="callout theorem-callout theorem-callout-proposition theorem-callout-en theorem-callout-mathwiki" data-callout="proposition" data-theorem-index="23.4">
<div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-icon"><svg class="svg-inline--fa" viewBox="0 0 448 512"><use href="#cicon-fa-calculator"/></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner"><span class="theorem-callout-main-title">Proposition 23.4</span> <span class="theorem-callout-subtitle">(Percentage)</span>.</div></div>
<div class="callout-content">

Percentage equals

$$
\text{percentage} = (\text{relative frequency}) \cdot 100\%
$$

</div>
</div>
