## Population and Sample {#population-and-sample}
  
<div class="callout theorem-callout theorem-callout-definition theorem-callout-en theorem-callout-mathwiki" data-callout="definition" data-theorem-index="22.4">
<div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-icon"><svg class="svg-inline--fa" viewBox="0 0 448 512"><use href="#cicon-fa-book"/></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner"><span class="theorem-callout-main-title">Definition 22.4</span> <span class="theorem-callout-subtitle">(Population or Target Population)</span>.</div></div>
<div class="callout-content">

A population consists of all elements whose characteristics are being studied  
The population that is being studied is also called the target population  

</div>
</div>

  
<div class="callout theorem-callout theorem-callout-definition theorem-callout-en theorem-callout-mathwiki" data-callout="definition" data-theorem-index="22.5">
<div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-icon"><svg class="svg-inline--fa" viewBox="0 0 448 512"><use href="#cicon-fa-book"/></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner"><span class="theorem-callout-main-title">Definition 22.5</span> <span class="theorem-callout-subtitle">(Sample)</span>.</div></div>
<div class="callout-content">

A portion of the population selected for study is referred to as a sample  

</div>
</div>

  
<div class="callout theorem-callout theorem-callout-definition theorem-callout-en theorem-callout-mathwiki" data-callout="definition" data-theorem-index="22.6">
<div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-icon"><svg class="svg-inline--fa" viewBox="0 0 448 512"><use href="#cicon-fa-book"/></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner"><span class="theorem-callout-main-title">Definition 22.6</span> <span class="theorem-callout-subtitle">(Census and Sample Survey)</span>.</div></div>
<div class="callout-content">

A survey that includes every member of the population is called a census  
The technique of collecting information from a portion of the population is called a sample survey  

</div>
</div>

<div class="callout-content">

A sample that represents the characteristics of the population as closely as possible is called a representative sample  

</div>
</div>

  
<div class="callout theorem-callout theorem-callout-definition theorem-callout-en theorem-callout-mathwiki" data-callout="definition" data-theorem-index="22.8">
<div class="callout-title"><div class="callout-icon"><svg class="svg-inline--fa" viewBox="0 0 448 512"><use href="#cicon-fa-book"/></svg></div><div class="callout-title-inner"><span class="theorem-callout-main-title">Definition 22.8</span> <span class="theorem-callout-subtitle">(Random Sample)</span>.</div></div>
<div class="callout-content">

A sample drawn such that each element of the population has a chance of being selected is called a random sample  
If all samples of the same size have the same chance of being selected, it is called simple random sampling  

</div>
</div>

<div class="callout-content">

A variable that cannot assume a numerical value but can be classified into categories is called a qualitative or categorical variable
