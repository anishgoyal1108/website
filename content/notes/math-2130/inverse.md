If $f: A \rightarrow B$ is a <a class="internal-link" href="/notes/math-2130/bijection/">bijection</a>, then the function $f^{-1}: B \rightarrow A$ defined by

$$f^{-1}(b) = a \iff f(a) = b$$

is called the inverse of $f$, denoted $f^{-1}$
