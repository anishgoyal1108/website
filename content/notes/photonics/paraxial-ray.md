- Incident light rays that form a small angle $\theta$ with the mirror axis, giving them special properties, like where they will (approximately) focus and image for certain geometries
	- Paraxial rays in the <a class="internal-link" href="/notes/photonics/spherical-mirror/">Spherical Mirror</a> are approximated by a <a class="internal-link" href="/notes/photonics/paraboloidal-mirror/">Paraboloidal Mirror</a> with a focus $-R/2$ from the center and vertex $-R/2$ from the focus (or $-R$ from the focus)
- Possible due to the small angle approximation
- Entire branch of optics dealing with paraxial rays, known as *paraxial optics/first-order/Gaussian* optics
