## Neutral Atom Arrays {#neutral-atom-arrays-1}
- An array of individual atoms with each atom confined in an "optical tweezer"
	- Each tweezer is a focused laser beam
- Atoms can be pushed using laser beams

<figure class="excalidraw"><a class="zoom" href="/notes/excalidraw/10-neutral-atom-arrays-2026-03-31-11-20-20.svg"><img src="/notes/excalidraw/10-neutral-atom-arrays-2026-03-31-11-20-20.svg" alt="10 Neutral Atom Arrays 2026-03-31 11.20.20" loading="lazy"></a></figure>

- $\delta$ is called the laser "detuning"
	- $\delta > 0$: blue-detuned; $\delta < 0$: red-detuned
- When laser is turned on, it shifts the ground-state energy by

$$\Delta E_{g} = \frac{\hbar \Omega_{R} \delta }{\delta ^2 + \frac{1}{2} \Gamma_{e}^2}$$

where $\Omega_R$ is the Rabi frequency
	- Frequency of atom cycling between $g$ and $e$

and $\Gamma_e$ is the spontaneous rate of emission
