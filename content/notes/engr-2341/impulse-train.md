An **impulse train** is a periodic sequence of Dirac delta functions, spaced at regular intervals:

$$
\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} \delta(t - nT_s)
$$

where $T_s$ is the **sampling period**, and $n$ is an integer that indexes the impulses at regular intervals.
