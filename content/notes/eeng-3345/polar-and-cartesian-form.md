## Complex Numbers {#complex-numbers}
- Cartesian: $z = x + jy$ (where $j$ is imaginary unit)
- Polar: $z = re^{j\theta} = r(\cos\theta + j\sin\theta)$
	- $x = r\cos\theta$
	- $y = rsin\theta$
	- $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$
	- $\theta = \arctan\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)$
- Deg to Rad: $\frac{\pi}{180}$
