## Concept {#concept}
- Dealing with funds that have different interests at different time periods

## Solution {#solution}
- Create huge "divider marks" that set the interest changes in the timeline
- Annuity payments cannot cross those divider lines without being considered another annuity entirely
- Be careful when accumulating/discounting interest across divider marks! You must have two separate stages if it goes past
