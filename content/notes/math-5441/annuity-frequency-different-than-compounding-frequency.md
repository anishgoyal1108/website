## Concept {#concept}
- We are dealing with annuities that compound interest at a different time interval than the fund's nominal interest rate

## How to Solve {#how-to-solve}
- Convert the nominal interest rate to the same time interval as the annuity; use that interest for all calculations

$$\left(1 + \frac{i^{(m)}}{m}\right)^{mt} = \left(1 + \frac{i^{(m^*)}}{m^*}\right)^{mt} $$
