## Compound Interest {#compound-interest-1}
- Interest earned at the end of a period is automatically reinvested at the start of the next period
- $i$ is the rate of compound interest

$$a(t) = (1+i)^t$$

- The effective rate of interest for compound interest is constant

## $\mathbb{R}^+$ Extension of Compound Interest {#r-extension-of-compound-interest}
- $a$ must satisfy:

$$a(t+s)=a(t)\cdot a(s)$$

## Relationship with Simple Interest {#relationship-with-simple-interest}
- Less if $t < 1$
- Equal if $t = 0,1$
- More if $t > 1$
