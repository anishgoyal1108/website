## Section-by-Section Index {#section-by-section-index}

Chapter 4 continues directly from Chapter 3's §18.2 (see <a class="internal-link" href="/notes/math-2243/chapter-3-study-guide/">Chapter 3's guide</a>, section 3.11). Two structural quirks worth knowing before you use this as a study order:

- **§18.3 "Exact Differentials" never gets its own course section.** Its core idea (the cross-derivative test) is folded into 4.2's coverage of §18.2 instead.
- **§18.6 "Flux and the Divergence Theorem" is taught in two separate halves out of book order**: 4.6 covers the *flux integral definition* (normally the first half of §18.6) *before* Stokes' Theorem is introduced in 4.7–4.8 (§18.5); then 4.9 comes back to finish §18.6 with the actual Divergence Theorem.
