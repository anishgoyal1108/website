## Section-by-Section Index {#section-by-section-index}

Course chapter 1 covers book §13.1–13.6 (Vectors) in order, then **jumps to §14.6–14.7** (Curves in Space) — §14.1, 14.2, 14.4, and 14.5 (conic sections, rotation of axes, quadric surfaces, cylindrical/spherical coordinates) are **not covered anywhere in this chapter's numbering**; they don't appear to get their own course section at all. Also note **1.6 and 1.7 are taught out of book order**: matrices/determinants (§13.6) comes *before* the cross product (§13.5).
